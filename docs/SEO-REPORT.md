## Step 0: what this repo is

- **Stack:** plain HTML marketing site at the repo root (141 pages) and an app built from `app/src` with esbuild, served
  as static files by Vercel (Part 13.2). Phone apps are Capacitor shells of the web app (`mobile/`).
- **Main domain:** `https://www.heytribe.app`. `heytribe.app` redirects to www (308), http redirects to https (308),
  HSTS is on. This matches the Part 2 table.
- **Languages:** English and Spanish. Part 2 says "check the repo"; it is English and Spanish.
- **Public pages:** home, features hub plus 18 feature pages, pricing, join, Hey Tribe assistant, about, contact, help,
  blog plus 3 posts, guides hub plus about 100 guides, printables, templates, chore generator, compare page, legal pages
  (privacy, terms, delete account, licenses), login.
- **Not public:** `/app/` (the app, sign-in and the sample family), `/admin/` and `admin.heytribe.app`, `/sitter/`
  (private sitter links), `/api/`, `thanks.html` and `404.html` (both already `noindex`).
- **Marketing site:** in this repo, so the whole pass was done here.
- **Repo rules vs playbook:** no conflicts. CLAUDE.md now also says to rebuild `es/` and the sitemap after page edits.
  One tension inside the playbook itself: Part 3.1 says to `Disallow: /app` while Part 3.3 says a page blocked in
  robots.txt can't show its `noindex`. I kept both on `/app/` and `/sitter/` (blocked from crawling, and `noindex` for
  any copy that gets in anyway), because the sitter links carry private tokens.

## Owner to-dos

1. **Search Console:** verify the domain property for heytribe.app (DNS), submit `https://www.heytribe.app/sitemap.xml`,
   and use URL Inspection on `/es/` and `/es/pricing.html` to ask for indexing. Then import the site into Bing
   Webmaster Tools.
2. **Rich Results Test:** paste the home page, `pricing.html` and one guide into search.google.com/test/rich-results
   and confirm no errors (my script found none).
3. **Spanish guides:** about 100 guides and the tools pages are mostly English with Spanish only for the menus. To give
   them Spanish pages, have someone translate and check the text in `i18n/es-site.json`, add the page to `ES_PAGES` in
   `tools/build-es.mjs`, and run `npm run build:es`. Until then they are English-only, which is the honest signal.
4. **Spanish legal pages:** `/es/privacy.html` and `/es/terms.html` are now indexable. Their Spanish text came from the
   existing dictionary; have it checked by the lawyer along with the English. I changed no legal wording.
5. **Children's privacy (Part 10):** the privacy policy has a "Children's information" section (accounts are for
   adults, kids use Kid view inside a parent's family). Have the lawyer confirm it covers COPPA and the state age laws.
6. **App stores:** once the apps are live, send me the App Store ID and Play package link. I'll add the Smart App Banner,
   the official badges and `sameAs` links. Store listing (ASO) suggestions: put "family calendar" and "chore chart" in
   the subtitle or short description, and lead the screenshots with the shared calendar and the kids' chore chart.
7. **Social profiles:** if HeyTribe has real Instagram, Facebook or similar pages, send them and I'll add them to the
   Organization `sameAs`. None are in the repo.
8. **Optional:** `http://heytribe.app` reaches www in two hops (http to https, then to www). This is Vercel's default
   and Google handles it; it can only be changed in Vercel's domain settings, not the repo.

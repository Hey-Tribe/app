Questions

Good questions, quick answers.

How do I get it on my phone?

Open www.heytribe.app/app on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap Install app. It works on tablets and computers too.

Does every family member need a phone?

No. Grown-ups can use their own phones, and kids can use Kid view on a shared tablet. It's a big, simple screen with just their routines and chores.

How do the rest of my family join?

Copy the invite from Family & settings and send it to them. They open the app, tap “Join with a code”, type the 6-character code and pick which person they are.

Can grandparents or the sitter join?

Yes, with your invite code. For a sitter who only needs the essentials, the Sitter sheet builds a care sheet with allergies, meds, bedtimes and emergency contacts that you can copy and send.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. Tap ES at the top of this site, or choose Español under Family & settings in the app. Each person picks their own language.

Who can see our family's information?

Anyone who joins with your tribe's invite code. Share it only with family and people you trust.

How much does it cost?

Every feature is free to use. Premium removes the limits and adds the extras, and every new tribe gets 7 days of it free. After that it's $4.99 a month for the whole family. See pricing

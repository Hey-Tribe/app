HeyTribe vs. Cozi, Google Calendar and shared notes

October 18, 20264 min read

The right tool depends on what your family needs to coordinate. Here is how the common options compare, including when HeyTribe is not the best fit.

Families usually start with something they already have: Google Calendar, a shared notes app or a long text thread. Some move to a family organizer like Cozi or HeyTribe. The right tool depends on what you need to coordinate. This page compares the approaches honestly, including when something else is the better choice for you.

We built HeyTribe, so keep that in mind. For other products, check their own sites for current features and pricing, since those change.

The short version

If you mostly needA good fit
A shared calendar for two adultsGoogle Calendar or any shared calendar. It is free, widely used and works with work calendars.

Google Calendar

Google Calendar is a strong calendar. You can share calendars, give each person a color and get reminders, and it works well alongside work calendars. It does not include chores, shopping lists tied to dinner plans, a baby log, a medicine log, school forms or a sitter sheet. Many families use it for plans and something else for the rest.

You can also subscribe to a HeyTribe calendar link from Google Calendar, so the two can work together.

Shared notes and checklists

A shared notes app works for lists and quick ideas, and you probably have one already. The tradeoff is that notes do not know about dates, people or due times. Nothing reminds the family about a form or a medicine dose, and everything depends on someone checking the note.

Group texts

Texts are quick, and that is the problem: information gets buried. “What time is practice?” becomes a scroll back through two weeks of messages. Texting is good for urgent changes, and a calendar or list is better for anything you will need again.

Cozi

Cozi is a long-running family organizer with a shared calendar and lists. If you are comparing it with HeyTribe, look at what each offers for your own situation: kids' screens and chore charts, health and medicine tracking, the baby log, the sitter sheet, how each handles reminders and what each costs. Check Cozi's own site for its current plans and features.

HeyTribe

HeyTribe brings together a shared calendar with a lane for each person, shopping lists, a dinner menu, chore charts, routines, Kid view, school forms, health and medicine, a baby log, bills, documents, trips, contacts and a sitter sheet. The Today screen sums up the day. Hey Tribe lets you type a sentence and see what it understood before adding it. Every feature is free to use, with a Premium plan at $4.99 a month for the whole tribe that goes deeper inside each one. You can read the details on the pricing page and in Free vs. Premium.

HeyTribe runs in a web browser and on your phone's home screen, and iPhone and Android store apps are coming soon.

When another tool is the better choice

  • You only need a calendar and both adults already live in Google Calendar.
  • Your family prefers paper, and a wall calendar works for you.
  • You need a tool your workplace requires.
  • You need a specific feature we do not have yet. We do not have two-way sync with Google or Apple calendars. You can subscribe to the HeyTribe calendar from them.

How to decide

List the five things your family drops most often. If they are all calendar items, a calendar is enough. If they include chores, forms, medicine, lists and rides, a family organizer will save more time. Whichever you pick, give it a month and check whether the whole family is really using it. The best tool is the one everybody opens.

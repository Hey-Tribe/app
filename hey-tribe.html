How it reads you

Who, when, and what.

Who

It knows everyone in your tribe by name, so "for Leo" lands in Leo's column and "Maria drives" sets the driver.

When

"Next Tuesday at 3", "tomorrow", "Saturday morning". It turns everyday words into a date and time.

What

Plans go on the calendar, groceries go on the list, jobs go on the chore chart and everything else goes on the fridge.

Routines

Morning routine

A school-day morning for kids who can follow a short list.

  1. Wake up and use the bathroom
  2. Get dressed
  3. Eat breakfast
  4. Brush teeth and hair
  5. Pack the school bag
  6. Shoes and jacket on
  7. Say goodbye and head out

After-school routine

A steady order for the hour after school.

  1. Bag on the hook and shoes off
  2. Snack and water
  3. Show a grown-up any forms or notes
  4. Homework or reading
  5. Get tomorrow's gear ready
  6. Free time

Bedtime routine

A calm sequence for younger kids. Adjust the order to fit your house.

  1. Tidy up toys
  2. Bath or wash up
  3. Pajamas on
  4. Brush teeth
  5. Pick two books
  6. Story and snuggles
  7. Lights out

Planning

Weekly family meeting agenda

A 15-minute agenda for a weekly look at what is coming. See the Sunday reset post for how it can run.

  1. Read the week out loud, day by day
  2. Decide who drives where
  3. Pick four or five dinners
  4. Add missing items to the grocery list
  5. Sign forms and note due dates
  6. One thing that went well this week
  7. One thing to change next week

Family overnight packing list

A base list for a weekend away. Add or remove items for your trip.

  1. Phone chargers
  2. Toothbrushes and toothpaste
  3. Pajamas
  4. Underwear and socks
  5. Clothes for each day, plus one extra outfit
  6. Jackets or rain gear
  7. Medicine and any prescriptions
  8. Diapers, wipes and a changing pad
  9. Comfort items and a favorite toy
  10. Snacks and water bottles
  11. Books, games or tablets with headphones
  12. Wallet, keys and ID
  13. Car seats and stroller
  14. Sunscreen and a hat

Seasonal

Holiday prep checklist

Work back from the date. This list covers the common jobs for a family holiday at home.

  1. Pick the date, the guest list and the budget
  2. Confirm who is hosting and who is bringing what
  3. Plan the menu and note any allergies
  4. Order or buy the main item early
  5. Make the grocery list and shop a few days ahead
  6. Wrap gifts and write cards
  7. Clean and set up the guest space
  8. Set the table the night before
  9. Plan the day's schedule, including nap and quiet times
  10. Prepare an easy meal for the day after

Planning

Kid birthday party planning

A checklist for a home or park party. Start about six weeks before.

  1. Pick the date, time and place
  2. Set the guest list and budget
  3. Send invitations and ask for RSVPs and allergies
  4. Choose a theme, or skip it
  5. Plan the food and order the cake
  6. Plan two or three activities and a backup for rain
  7. Buy decorations and goodie bags
  8. Confirm the headcount a few days ahead
  9. Pack the party kit: candles, lighter, plates, napkins, trash bags
  10. Assign helpers for setup, food and cleanup
  11. Keep a list of gifts and who gave them

School

Back-to-school checklist

The jobs that come up at the start of a school year or term.

  1. Add the school calendar and days off to the family calendar
  2. Return forms, emergency contacts and medical forms
  3. Buy the supply list
  4. Check uniforms, shoes and backpacks
  5. Save teacher and office contacts
  6. Set up the morning and after-school routines
  7. Plan lunches and note any allergies
  8. Pick up and drop-off plan, with a backup driver
  9. Check bus or carpool schedules
  10. Mark picture day, parent night and the first long weekend

Care

Sitter handoff checklist

What to leave for a babysitter, grandparent or other caregiver.

  1. Your phone numbers and a backup contact
  2. Address of the house and the nearest cross street
  3. Allergies and any medicine, with times and amounts
  4. Doctor and emergency numbers
  5. Bedtime routine and what each child likes
  6. Food, snack and screen rules
  7. Wi-Fi name and password
  8. Where the first aid kit and flashlight are
  9. When you expect to be home
  10. Anything that is off limits

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

  1. 1

    Add chores

    Name the job, choose whose it is and which days.

  2. 2

    Set a reward

    Let each kid choose the prize and the star goal.

  3. 3

    Stamp and celebrate

    Stars fill the jar as the week goes on.

Good questions.

Do stars reset?

Each week starts a fresh chart, and the jar counts that week's stars.

Can grown-ups have chores too?

Yes. Anyone in the tribe can have a chart.

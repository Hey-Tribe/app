Pricing questions

Do we pay anything right now?

No. Payments aren't switched on yet, so every feature, Premium included, is free during early access. Premium billing starts at launch.

Does Premium cover the whole family?

Yes. One Premium plan covers everyone in your tribe, grown-ups and kids.

Can we start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Everything you add on Free stays put when you upgrade.

What happens if we go back to Free?

Everything you added stays. Each feature goes back to its Free version, so extras like repeating events, reminders and the medicine log pause until you upgrade again.
